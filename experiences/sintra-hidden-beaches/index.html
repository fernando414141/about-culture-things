Why this day

Leave the coach route for the Atlantic rhythm.

This experience gives Sintra and its coastline room to breathe. It starts with the historic and mountain landscape, then follows the local road toward Praia das Maçãs, Azenhas do Mar and Praia da Aguda.

Every traveller has an assigned seat in a nine-seat Mercedes-Benz Vito. It is a luxury van, not a bus, with a local guide and dedicated driver travelling as separate professionals.

Best suited toFamilies, repeat visitors and travellers who prefer a coastal focus within a five-hour shared tour.

How the day unfolds

The shared route moves from Sintra’s historic centre and mountain viewpoints toward villages and beaches on the Atlantic.

  1. Pickup at a confirmed Lisbon meeting point or accommodation
  2. Sintra Historic Center and the National Palace area
  3. Castelo dos Mouros and Serra de Sintra viewpoints
  4. Pena Palace viewpoint
  5. Praia das Maçãs
  6. Azenhas do Mar and Praia da Aguda
