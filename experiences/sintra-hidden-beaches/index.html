Why this day

Trade the group timetable for the Atlantic rhythm.

This experience gives Sintra and its coastline room to breathe. It starts with the historic and mountain landscape, then follows the local road toward Praia das Maçãs, Azenhas do Mar and Praia da Aguda.

With a shorter duration and a coastal focus, it avoids trying to turn every landmark into a checklist.

Best suited toFamilies, repeat visitors and travellers who prefer a slower coastal pace within a five-hour private tour.

How the day unfolds

The sequence moves from Sintra’s historic centre and mountain viewpoints toward villages and beaches on the Atlantic.

  1. Pickup at your hotel or accommodation in Lisbon
  2. Sintra Historic Center and the National Palace area
  3. Castelo dos Mouros and Serra de Sintra viewpoints
  4. Pena Palace viewpoint
  5. Praia das Maçãs
  6. Azenhas do Mar and Praia da Aguda
