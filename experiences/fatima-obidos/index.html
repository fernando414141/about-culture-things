Why this day

Two distinct places, connected with time and context.

Óbidos and Fátima offer very different encounters with Portuguese heritage. The private format removes the shared-coach timetable and allows each place to hold its own character: the medieval fabric of Óbidos, then the scale and meaning of the Fátima Sanctuary.

Pickup, transport and the return to Lisbon are organised as one full day.

Best suited toTravellers interested in Portuguese heritage, medieval atmosphere and the cultural or spiritual significance of Fátima.

How the day unfolds

A direct private route from Lisbon connects Óbidos and Fátima, with the day’s logistics and return already resolved.

  1. Pickup at your hotel or accommodation in Lisbon
  2. Travel to the medieval village of Óbidos
  3. Time among the village’s historic lanes
  4. Continue to Fátima
  5. Visit the Fátima Sanctuary
  6. Private return and drop-off in Lisbon
