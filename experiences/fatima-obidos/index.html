Why this day

Two distinct places, connected with time and context.

Óbidos and Fátima offer very different encounters with Portuguese heritage. The small shared format gives each place room to hold its own character: the medieval fabric of Óbidos, then the scale and meaning of the Fátima Sanctuary.

Every traveller has an assigned seat in a nine-seat Mercedes-Benz Vito. It is a luxury van, not a bus, with a local guide and dedicated driver travelling as separate professionals.

Best suited toTravellers interested in Portuguese heritage, medieval atmosphere and the cultural or spiritual significance of Fátima.

How the day unfolds

A direct shared route from Lisbon connects Óbidos and Fátima, with the day’s logistics and return already resolved.

  1. Pickup at a confirmed Lisbon meeting point or accommodation
  2. Travel to the medieval village of Óbidos
  3. Time among the village’s historic lanes
  4. Continue to Fátima
  5. Visit the Fátima Sanctuary
  6. Shared return and drop-off in Lisbon
